A card game we play in person, rebuilt so it works with friends across countries.

  • Authoritative server, so no one can cheat
  • Reconnect and spectators via a per-tab identity
  • Illustrated deck, sound, installable app
Explore this project → Play the live game ↗
Rules engine, play a trick
You hold the Ace of Spades, so you lead. Pick a card.

Highest card of the lead suit loses the trick. The moment someone plays off-suit, the trick ends and the loser picks up the whole pile.

Follow suit where you can, invalid cards are disabled
